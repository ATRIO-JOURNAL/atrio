Atrio is an international, multidisciplinary peer-reviewed journal committed to publishing high-quality, original research across all academic disciplines. We provide a platform for researchers, scholars, and professionals to share their findings and foster global academic exchange.

Whether you belong to the field of science, technology, humanities, social sciences, or any other academic area, our journal offers the visibility your work deserves.

Key Details

  • Journal Title: Atrio Journal
  • ISSN: 0214-8293
  • Email: submit@atriojournal.com
  • Publication Type: Peer-Reviewed Journal
  • Access Type: Open Access
  • Review Process: Double-Blind Peer Review
  • Frequency: Regular / Continuous Publication
  • Scope: Multidisciplinary
  • Index: Scopus, Web of Science, Google Scholar, Crossref DOI, Research Gate, ERIH PLUS and Latindex

Scope and Areas Covered

Atrio Journal adopts a multidisciplinary approach, welcoming original research articles, review papers, and case studies in diverse fields including:

  • Engineering and Technology
  • Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence
  • Management and Commerce
  • Social Sciences and Humanities
  • Arts and Literature
  • Education and Pedagogy
  • Medical and Health Sciences
  • Environmental and Agricultural Sciences
  • Law and Public Policy
  • Applied Sciences and Interdisciplinary Research

The journal encourages cross-disciplinary collaboration, promoting research that integrates multiple fields to address real-world challenges.

Peer Review and Quality

Atrio follows a double-blind peer review process, ensuring academic rigor and quality in all published articles. It is indexed in various academic databases and recognized in systems such as ERIH PLUS and Latindex, confirming its scholarly credibility.

Aim and Objectives

  • To provide a global research platform for all disciplines
  • To promote innovative and interdisciplinary research
  • To ensure quality and integrity through rigorous peer review
  • To support academic and professional development of researchers worldwide

Peer Review and Publication Ethics

Atrio Journal follows a strict double-blind peer review process to maintain high academic standards. All submissions are evaluated based on originality, relevance, methodology, and contribution to knowledge.

The journal adheres to standard publication ethics, ensuring transparency, plagiarism-free content, and unbiased editorial decisions.

Open Access Policy

Atrio Journal follows an open-access model, allowing readers to access all published articles freely. This ensures maximum visibility, accessibility, and impact of research without barriers.

Conclusion

Atrio Journal (ISSN: 0214-8293) stands as a multidisciplinary research platform, fostering innovation and knowledge exchange across various academic domains. It aims to bridge gaps between disciplines and contribute to the advancement of global research and development.
